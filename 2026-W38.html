Japanese manufacturer opens regional headquarters in HCMC

  • A Japanese manufacturing occupier committed to 2,400 sqm of Grade B office space in District 3.
  • The company plans to hire 150 engineers over the next two years as it expands its design operations in Vietnam.
  • Brokers said demand from Japanese occupiers has been steady this year, with most requirements between 300 and 1,500 sqm.

HCMC attracts US$2.1 billion in FDI in first eight months

  • HCMC attracted US$2.1 billion in FDI in the first eight months of 2026, up 14% y-o-y.
  • Manufacturing and professional services led new registrations.
  • Singaporean and Japanese investors were the largest sources of new capital in the period, the office said, adding that several regional headquarters were registered in District 1 and Thu Thiem.
